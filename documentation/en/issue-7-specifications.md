## 1. Context and objectives

The issue reports empty diagnostics and a stopped integration after changing a parameter, without a confirmed local reproduction. The **reported path** is the gear icon “Configure”, opening **Options** on a per-VTherm entry. Reconfigure and the global entry are coverage paths, not confirmed failures. The goal is to apply new settings at **Pellet Stove** reload while preserving the user-visible cycle and diagnostics, without modifying or reloading the VTherm entry or configuration. Pellet Stove may trigger a justified transition under its own control at that reload, without an HA restart. No internal mechanism is prescribed.

## 2. Actors and use cases

| Actor / case                      | Trigger and normal flow                                                                   | Expected result                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| --------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| HA administrator, global settings | Edits a global entry through Options or Reconfigure; inspects affected VTherms.           | Only VTherms without a specific entry depend on global settings; this is coverage, not a reported failure.                                                                                                |
| HA administrator, local settings  | Opens Configure/Options on a per-VTherm entry (reported path), or Reconfigure (coverage). | Only the targeted VTherm uses its complete specific configuration, without global values, including under Options.                                                                                        |
| Occupant and controlled stove     | Changes settings during heating or an off phase.                                          | The user-visible cycle remains consistent at Pellet Stove reload except for justified transitions; a newly breached safety threshold requires priority shutdown, including when a command is in progress. |
| Operator / diagnostics            | Views the sensor after applying settings, including on resumption failure.                | Known phase, state and deadline remain consistent; no unjustified historical debug values or false restoration.                                                                                           |

## 3. Functional requirements

| ID     | Verifiable requirement                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| ------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| FR-001 | The system shall accept changes to existing parameters through Options and Reconfigure, for global and per-VTherm entries, without requiring reinstallation.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| FR-002 | The system shall apply a VTherm's entire specific configuration when one exists, without mixing in global values, including under Options; without a specific entry it shall use global settings or defaults under BR-001. Global changes shall not change effective values for VTherms with a specific entry.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| FR-003 | The system shall preserve known ON/OFF state (`is_heating`), known `last_on_at` and `last_off_at`, and elapsed guard time; at reload only a justified confirmed transition or BR-004/005 priorities may change this state without erasing history. Known valid power index, reason and boost shall remain consistent; runtime identity is not required.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| FR-004 | At Pellet Stove reload the system shall keep the cycle and diagnostics consistent from the user's perspective except for effects justified by new settings or safety; it shall neither restart delays from the edit time nor show unjustified debug values. Exact scheduler/runtime identity is not required.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| FR-005 | The system shall recalculate `next_action_allowed_at`, when a relevant timestamp exists, from that timestamp and the new effective durations under BR-002 and BR-003, then make it visible; if there is no relevant timestamp, it may remain absent.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| FR-006 | At Pellet Stove reload with new settings the system shall perform under its own control any actually required and permitted ON/OFF transition without spurious or duplicate commands or an HA restart; if an ON/OFF command is already in progress, it shall let that command finish before resuming the cycle and re-evaluating the new settings, except when safety shutdown takes priority. A finished command whose outcome is unknown shall not be presumed successful. If a new safety threshold is already breached, the system shall prioritize shutdown even during minimum heating time; this exception prescribes neither a technical means of interrupting an in-flight command nor hardware atomicity. |
| FR-007 | The system shall retain or restore an accessible diagnostic sensor for each affected VTherm after reconfiguration, with state, active-parameter and next-deadline attributes updated, no visible duplicate and no persistently empty values for a known state.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| FR-008 | The system shall prevent an older saved state from replacing the latest confirmed state. If preservation, saving or restoration fails, it shall still apply the new settings, log the error and not claim successful restoration; if the physical state becomes unknown, it shall send no automatic ignition command, but a safety shutdown remains permitted. It shall not infer physical state merely because a command with an unknown outcome has finished.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| FR-009 | The system shall remain usable after reconfiguring the sole global entry and retain diagnostics; it shall neither modify nor reload VTherm, its configuration or its entry as a result of Pellet Stove Options or Reconfigure, whether global or local.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| FR-010 | Before delivery the system shall describe this behavior consistently in FR/EN guides: local/global scope, cycle, deadlines, safety, no VTherm reload and failure reporting; the guides are not changed here.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |

## 4. Business rules

| ID     | Condition, exception and priority                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| ------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| BR-001 | A VTherm-specific entry shall **entirely** replace global configuration, including under Options: absent keys shall not be filled from global settings. Without a specific entry global configuration applies. Defaults may complete the chosen source, but global values shall never complete a specific entry; internal Options/data/default precedence shall not reintroduce global values.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| BR-002 | While heating, the deadline for leaving the minimum-duration guard is calculated from `last_on_at` using the new minimum heating duration; this deadline does not itself order shutdown.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| BR-003 | While off, the earliest permitted reignition is calculated from `last_off_at` using the sum of the new minimum-off and cooldown durations; this deadline does not itself order ignition.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| BR-004 | An explicitly OFF HVAC mode and an already breached safety threshold shall override minimum heating time; a **new** threshold already breached shall prioritize shutdown at reload even during that minimum and while a command is in progress. Outside this priority, an ON/OFF command already in progress shall finish before the cycle resumes and new settings are re-evaluated; completion does not prove success if the outcome is unknown. With unknown physical state after resumption failure, automatic ignition is forbidden but safety shutdown remains permitted. An expired deadline alone is not a transition request. |
| BR-005 | Reconfiguration is not a voluntary history reset; when restored state contradicts an explicitly off HVAC mode, consistency with off mode takes precedence without artificially resetting `last_off_at`.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| BR-006 | Without a relevant known timestamp, no historical deadline or allegedly restored runtime value shall be invented for diagnostics.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |

## 5. Functional constraints

- Compatibility: existing entries, per-thermostat configurations, sensor IDs and stored history shall remain usable; local/global separation changes the current effective values of partial local entries (DIV-001), to be checked before delivery.
- Operational safety: avoid spurious or duplicate commands; prioritize shutdown at reload when the new threshold is already breached, even during minimum heating time or an in-flight command. With unknown physical state after resumption failure, no automatic ignition; safety shutdown remains permitted. Neither technical interruption of an in-flight command nor hardware atomicity is guaranteed; feasibility and observable command ordering require tests and a command contract.
- Availability and experience: regulation and diagnostics remain usable after Pellet Stove reload without reinstallation or VTherm entry reload. Maximum outage duration: unknown.
- Privacy / regulation: no new personal data or regulatory requirement identified in the sources; do not expose additional sensitive data in diagnostics or logs.

If a finished command has no known outcome, neither its success nor the resulting physical state is presumed (Q-003).                                                                                                                                                                                           |

## 7. Out-of-scope features

- Changes to the stove command protocol or VTherm/Duepi integrations, or a new configuration UI: not needed for the issue described in the review.
- Voluntary history reset: distinct from reconfiguration.
- A guarantee of scheduler/runtime identity or a maximum switchover duration: unnecessary for observable continuity; duration unknown.
- Requiring an HA restart as a condition for a justified transition: excluded by the user clarification; such a restart remains an unconfirmed workaround, not a functional fix.
- Modifying or reloading VTherm, its configuration or its entry because of Pellet Stove Options or Reconfigure: explicitly excluded. Guaranteeing hardware atomicity or prescribing an interruption mechanism for in-flight commands: outside the required functional outcome; contract and feasibility remain to be established (Q-003).

## 8. Proposed future evolution

- After resolving the incident, consider an explicit “settings applied” indication and non-sensitive resumption traceability; usability and value to be confirmed.
- Evaluate a policy for actions outside reload and absent runtime fields; these are not current requirements.

## 9. Assumptions, open questions and traceability
**Technical checkpoint (2026-10-24):** the simulated local Options path in `tests/test_services.py` reproduces failure to apply new settings to the live handler (10 remains active after 42 is saved); diagnostic attributes remain present but stale. Empty diagnostics and actual control failure remain unreproduced. Without a global entry, unloading the last plugin entry still reloads VTherm. `vtherm_api` 0.7.0 and the scheduler do not establish confirmed command outcome/physical state or OFF priority during an in-flight command; implementation stops at this blocker, without commanding a real stove. See the feasibility addendum in the [design](../tech-docs/issue-7-design.md).

**Verified facts / gaps:** the validated review relays empty diagnostics and stopped operation as *reported*, not reproduced. The [user clarification](../tech-docs/issue-7-review.md) resolves “restart” in [issue #7](https://github.com/jmcollin78/vtherm_pellet_stove/issues/7): it means integration reload with the new parameters, not a mandatory HA restart. `PelletStoveConfigFlow.async_step_reconfigure` updates data and clears options; `PelletStoveOptionsFlow.async_step_init` saves options. `async_setup_entry`, `_async_update_options` and `async_unload_entry` in [`__init__.py`](../../custom_components/vtherm_pellet_stove/__init__.py) trigger reloads and manage the diagnostic platform; `_resolve_options`, `init_algorithm`, `async_added_to_hass`, `async_startup`, `remove` and `control_heating` in [`handler.py`](../../custom_components/vtherm_pellet_stove/handler.py) read options, restore and control state. `get_debug_attributes` in [`controller.py`](../../custom_components/vtherm_pellet_stove/pellet/controller.py) computes the deadline; `PelletState.save_state/restore_state` in [`state.py`](../../custom_components/vtherm_pellet_stove/pellet/state.py) do not save complete scheduler state or the last decision; `PelletDebugSensor.update_from_controller` in [`sensor.py`](../../custom_components/vtherm_pellet_stove/sensor.py) publishes diagnostics. The FR/EN user guides currently promise automatic VTherm reload in section 5.3; this describes current behavior without establishing each reload's outcome. Tests [`test_handler.py`](../../tests/test_handler.py) and [`test_services.py`](../../tests/test_services.py) verify fragments, not the complete HA scenario with unloaded platform, in-flight save and preserved cycle.

**Assumptions and limits:** H-001, continuity means observable behavior, not scheduler identity (now decided in FR-004). H-002, a past deadline means permission, not an ON/OFF demand. The review's causal hypotheses (sensor reused after platform unload, temporary factory unavailability, late save) remain unproven; local reproduction has been offered, not demonstrated. Only Configure/Options on a VTherm-specific entry is the reported path; Reconfigure/global are coverage.

**Settled decisions:** D-001, user-visible cycle and diagnostics remain continuous at Pellet Stove reload except for justified effects of settings or safety, without requiring runtime identity; D-002, expired deadline indicates permission, not a command. Pellet Stove may control a justified transition at its reload; VTherm, its configuration and its entry are neither modified nor reloaded.

**DIV-001 (settled; current code differs):** a specific entry completely replaces global configuration even for absent keys and under Options; Options/data/default precedence *within the same entry* must never reintroduce global values. `_resolve_options` currently selects `entry.options or entry.data` per entry, then overlays local on global: not compliant for partial local entries. [Design §4/5/9](../tech-docs/issue-7-design.md) now selects one entry and fills missing keys from defaults, without global inheritance; internal precedence and partial Options require technical verification.

**DIV-002 (settled for observable outcome):** Store v1 serializes `is_heating`, `current_level_index`, `last_on_at`, `last_off_at`, `last_reason` and `boost_until`; known valid data and guards remain consistent under FR-003. Energy accounting, complete last decision (`on_percent`), scheduler state and resumption generation are not persisted: exact runtime identity or invented historical values are not required. Diagnostics shall avoid unjustified debug values; feasibility remains to be demonstrated.

**DIV-003 (closed):** review approved on 2026-10-23 for specification/design; design v0.5 aligns with the 2026-10-24 decisions and still needs conditional approval, implementation not authorized. Decisions settle DIV-001, DIV-002, Q-001, the safety case of Q-002 and Q-003's functional outcome; Q-004 still needs evidence.

**FR/EN functional convergence, conditional feasibility:** FR-001..010, BR-001..006 and AC-001..010 define the expected outcome. Design v0.5 rejects B (VTherm reload) and proposes A (updating live handlers during Pellet Stove reload, without VTherm reload) without claiming proven feasibility. Q-003 is functionally aligned in §3/5/6/8/9: unknown physical state, in-flight commands and priority safety shutdown are covered, without promising technical interruption or hardware atomicity. HA must establish the live-handler/scheduler update and control contract, safe threshold shutdown even with no target and during an in-flight command, knowledge of command outcome, save/resume ordering, active sensor registration after global unload, factory availability and command counts. Outcomes require validation through AC-001..010 with frozen time, simulated stove services, injected errors and actual HA lifecycle paths; feasibility depends on those contracts and tests, without VTherm modification/reload. Implementation is not authorized.

**Settled decision and remaining questions before acceptance:**

- Q-001 (**closed**): Pellet Stove reload with new settings, justified transition under its control, without HA restart or VTherm entry/configuration modification or reload.
- Q-002 (**settled for the new threshold at reload**): if already breached, priority shutdown without waiting for minimum heating time; with an in-flight command apply the priority under Q-003, subject to command-contract feasibility. Timing of actions outside reload is not specified here.
- Q-003 (**functionally settled on 2026-10-24; feasibility to validate**): despite preservation/save/restore failure, apply new settings, log the error and do not claim successful restoration; if physical state becomes unknown, no automatic ignition, safety shutdown permitted. An ON/OFF command already in progress finishes before the cycle resumes and new settings are re-evaluated, except for priority safety shutdown. Completion does not prove success when outcome is unknown. Factual questions: how can command outcome and physical state be observed, and how can safety shutdown be prioritized during an in-flight command under the available stove/HA contract? No interruption mechanism or hardware atomicity is presumed.
- Q-004 (**path identified, evidence missing**): Configure/Options on a VTherm-specific entry is reported; Reconfigure/global are coverage. HA/VTherm/plugin versions, logs, before/after state and local/HA reproduction are not yet demonstrated.
